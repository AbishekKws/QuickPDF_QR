What you can do with QuickPDF QR

  • PDF to QR code. Upload one or a batch of PDFs and get a scannable code in seconds — no account, no app install.
  • One link, every file. The scanner opens a download page listing each PDF, with a single ZIP button for the whole batch.
  • Image to PDF. Turn JPG, PNG or WebP photos into a tidy A4 PDF, one page per image, with EXIF rotation fixed.
  • Excel, CSV and text to PDF. Spreadsheets become a readable table and notes become a clean document.
  • PDF back to images, Word, Excel or text. Save every page as a JPG, or pull the content out as an editable DOCX, XLSX or TXT file.
  • Original file names. Downloads keep the name you uploaded — no renamed or random files.
  • Nepali text supported. Devanagari and Latin text both render correctly in generated PDFs.

Frequently asked questions

How do I share a PDF with a QR code?

Choose your PDFs, press “Upload & generate QR”, and a QR code appears instantly. Print it, put it on a notice board or send it in chat — whoever scans it opens a download page with every file and a ZIP of the whole batch.

Can I convert images, Excel or text into a PDF here?

Yes. Pick Image, Excel/CSV or Text in the converter, choose your files and you get a PDF plus a QR code. JPG, PNG, WebP, XLSX, XLSM, CSV, TXT and Markdown are supported.

Can I turn a PDF back into images, Word or Excel?

Yes. In the reverse converter, pick Images, Text, Word or Excel and choose a PDF (up to 20 pages). You get one JPG per page, or a TXT, DOCX or XLSX file with the extracted text — shared through the same QR download page.

Is QuickPDF QR free, and do I need an account?

It is completely free and needs no account or app. Each batch can be up to 4 MB in total, which keeps uploads fast on mobile data.

How long are my files and QR links available?

Uploads and generated PDFs are deleted automatically after about 30 minutes, so download or save anything you need before the link expires.

Do converted PDFs support Nepali (Devanagari) text?

Yes. Converted PDFs bundle Devanagari and Latin fonts, so Nepali and English text both appear correctly instead of turning into empty boxes.
