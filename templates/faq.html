Frequently asked questions

Everything about limits, formats and what happens to your files after the link expires.

How do I share a PDF with a QR code?

Open PDF → QR, choose your PDFs and press “Upload & generate QR”. A QR code appears instantly. Print it, put it on a notice board or send it in chat — whoever scans it opens a download page with every file and a ZIP of the whole batch.

Can I convert images, Excel or text into a PDF here?

Yes. Open Anything → PDF, pick Image, Excel/CSV or Text, choose your files and you get a PDF plus a QR code. JPG, PNG, WebP, XLSX, XLSM, CSV, TXT and Markdown are supported.

Can I turn a PDF back into images, Word or Excel?

Yes. In PDF → anything, pick Images, Text, Word or Excel and choose a PDF (up to 20 pages). You get one JPG per page, or a TXT, DOCX or XLSX file with the extracted text — shared through the same QR download page.

Why does text extraction fail on some PDFs?

Text, Word and Excel need a real text layer. A PDF made from scans or photos has none, so there is nothing to copy. Use the Images option for those — it renders the pages and always works.

Is QuickPDF QR free, and do I need an account?

It is completely free and needs no account or app. Each batch can be up to {{ config.MAX_CONTENT_LENGTH // 1048576 }} MB in total, which keeps uploads fast on mobile data.

How long are my files and QR links available?

Uploads and generated files are deleted automatically after about {{ config.FILE_TTL_MINUTES }} minutes, so download or save anything you need before the link expires. After that the page shows “This link has expired.”

Do converted PDFs support Nepali (Devanagari) text?

Yes. Converted PDFs bundle Devanagari and Latin fonts, so Nepali and English text both appear correctly instead of turning into empty boxes.

Are my files private or searchable?

Download pages and file links are marked noindex, so search engines do not list them, and nothing is ever shown publicly. Still, treat the QR code like a shared link: anyone who has it can download the files until it expires.
